# CP-200 Pump Operation and Maintenance Manual (Excerpt)

## 1 Specifications

The CP-200 is a single-stage end-suction centrifugal pump driven by a 75 kW induction motor at 2960 rpm. Rated duty is 120 m³/h at 6.5 bar discharge pressure. Rated motor current is 42 A at full load. Bearings are grease-lubricated rolling element bearings with a water-cooled bearing housing.

## 2 Instrumentation

TT-101 drive-end bearing temperature (alarm 85 °C), TT-102 bearing cooling water return temperature (alarm 38 °C), FT-101 discharge flow (low alarm 60 m³/h), PT-101 discharge pressure (low alarm 3.0 bar, high alarm 9.0 bar), IT-101 motor current (high alarm 55 A), VT-101 bearing vibration velocity (alarm 7.1 mm/s).

## 3 Lubrication

Re-grease the drive-end bearing every 2000 running hours with 15 g of lithium-complex EP2 grease. Over-greasing raises bearing temperature for several hours; do not add grease outside the interval unless the lubrication record shows a missed interval.

## 4 Vibration Limits

Vibration is assessed against ISO 10816 for machines of this class: below 2.8 mm/s is good, 2.8–7.1 mm/s is acceptable for continued operation with trending, and above 7.1 mm/s requires action. A steady rise over days at constant load indicates bearing degradation.

## 5 Bearing Cooling Water System

The bearing housing is cooled by plant cooling water at 1.2–2.0 m³/h supplied through a manual supply valve and a Y-strainer. Return water passes TT-102 before rejoining the cooling water header. Loss or restriction of cooling water raises the return water temperature first and the bearing temperature shortly after.
