# CL-50 Cooling Water Loop — Plate Heat Exchanger Fouling

## 1 Scope

Applies to CL-50 closed cooling water loops with a plate heat exchanger rejecting heat to the site cooling tower circuit.

## 2 Normal Operation

Supply temperature TT-201 is 22–26 °C with a high alarm at 32 °C. Return temperature TT-202 is 29–33 °C. Loop flow FT-201 is about 300 m³/h and loop pressure PT-201 about 3.2 bar. The return-minus-supply temperature difference is normally 6–8 °C.

## 3 Fouling Symptoms

Heat exchanger fouling develops over days. Supply temperature TT-201 rises steadily while loop flow FT-201 and pressure PT-201 stay normal. Because the exchanger removes less heat, the return-minus-supply temperature difference narrows (for example from 7 °C to 3 °C). A sudden rise within minutes points instead to loss of cooling tower water or a stopped tower fan.

## 4 Actions

Confirm cooling tower water flow and temperature on the secondary side. If the secondary side is normal, the exchanger is fouled: switch to the standby exchanger if installed, then backflush the plates. If backflushing does not restore the temperature difference, open the exchanger and chemically clean the plates.

## 5 Return to Service

Return the exchanger to service and confirm TT-201 below 26 °C at normal load within two hours.
