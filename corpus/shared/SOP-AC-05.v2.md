# AC-75 Compressor High Discharge Temperature

## 1 Scope

Applies to AC-75 oil-injected rotary screw air compressors when the discharge temperature alarm TT-301 (105 °C) is active or the temperature trend is rising.

## 2 Safety

High discharge temperature can ignite oil vapour. Never bypass or disable the high discharge temperature trip. Isolate and depressurise the compressor before opening the cooler or oil circuit.

## 3 Normal Operation

Discharge temperature TT-301 is 85–92 °C at full load. Discharge pressure PT-301 is about 7.5 bar and motor current IT-301 about 120 A.

## 4 Diagnosis

### 4.1 Cooler Fan Failure

Pattern: TT-301 rises over one to a few hours while motor current IT-301 and discharge pressure PT-301 stay normal. The oil/air cooler is not rejecting heat. Check that the cooler fan is running, the fan motor overload has not tripped, and the fan belt is intact.

### 4.2 Blocked Cooler

Pattern: slow rise over weeks, worse on hot days. Inspect and clean the cooler fins with compressed air from the inside out.

### 4.3 Low Oil Level or Thermostatic Valve Fault

Pattern: rise together with low oil level in the sight glass. Top up with the specified compressor oil. If the oil level is correct, test the thermostatic valve; replace it if it does not open fully at its rated temperature.

## 5 Corrective Actions

Repair or replace the fan motor or belt, clean the cooler, or replace the thermostatic valve as diagnosed. Record the cause in the maintenance log.

## 6 Return to Service

Run the compressor loaded for one hour and confirm TT-301 stabilises below 95 °C.
