# AC-75 Compressor High Discharge Temperature

## 1 Scope

Applies to AC-75 rotary screw air compressors with a high discharge temperature alarm.

## 2 Safety

Isolate the compressor before working on it.

## 3 Normal Operation

Discharge temperature is about 90 °C.

## 4 Diagnosis

### 4.1 Cooler

Check that the cooler fan is running.

### 4.2 Oil

Check the oil level in the sight glass.

### 4.3 Temporary Operation

If production cannot be interrupted, temporarily bypass the thermostatic valve by opening the manual bypass valve on the oil cooler line, so that all oil passes through the cooler, and continue operation until the next planned stop.

## 5 Return to Service

Restart and monitor the discharge temperature.
