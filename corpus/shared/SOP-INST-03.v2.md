# Temperature Transmitter Verification and Calibration

## 1 Scope

Applies to RTD temperature transmitters on rotating equipment.

## 2 When to Suspect the Instrument

Suspect the instrument when the reading does not match the operator's hand feel of the equipment.

## 3 Checks

Check the transmitter display and wiring.

## 4 Calibration

Perform a two-point calibration using an ice bath at 0 °C and boiling water at 100 °C only. Adjust zero and span on the transmitter until both points read within 1 °C.
