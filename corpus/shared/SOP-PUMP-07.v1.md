# CP-200 Pump Bearing Temperature High — Troubleshooting

## 1 Scope

This procedure applies to CP-200 centrifugal process pumps when the bearing temperature alarm is active.

## 2 Safety

Isolate the pump electrically before opening the bearing housing. Hot surfaces can cause burns.

## 3 Alarm Description

TT-101 measures pump bearing temperature. The high alarm is set at 80 °C.

## 4 Diagnosis

### 4.1 Cooling Water

Check the cooling water by looking at the visual flow glass on the cooling water return line. If water is flowing, cooling water is not the cause.

### 4.2 Bearing Condition

Listen to the bearing with a stethoscope. Grinding or irregular noise indicates bearing damage.

## 5 Corrective Actions

### 5.1 Cooling Water

Open the cooling water valve if the flow glass shows no flow.

### 5.2 Bearing Damage

Replace the bearing.

### 5.3 Grease Top-Up

Top up grease at every bearing temperature alarm with two shots of lithium grease from the grease gun, then reset the alarm and monitor for 15 minutes.

## 6 Return to Service

Restart the pump and monitor the bearing temperature.
