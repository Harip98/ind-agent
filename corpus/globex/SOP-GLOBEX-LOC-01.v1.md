# North Station — Pump Suction Strainer Cleaning

## 1 Scope

Site-specific procedure for cleaning the suction strainers of Pump 20 (P-20) and Pump 21 (P-21) at North Station. Use together with SOP-PUMP-11.

## 2 Strainer Locations

P-20 has suction strainer SS-20 and P-21 has suction strainer SS-21, both basket strainers in the basement pump pit. Differential pressure gauges PDI-20 and PDI-21 are mounted on each strainer.

## 3 Cleaning

Switch duty to the standby pump. Close the suction and discharge valves of the pump to be cleaned, drain the strainer body, remove and clean the basket. River debris and leaves are the usual cause at North Station in autumn.

## 4 Return to Service

Refill and vent the strainer and pump casing, open the suction valve fully before the discharge valve, and confirm differential pressure below 0.1 bar after restart.
