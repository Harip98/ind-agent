# Plant A — Pump 12 Bearing Cooling Water Supply: Local Isolation and Flushing

## 1 Scope

Site-specific details for the bearing cooling water supply to Pump 12 (P-12) at Plant A. Use together with SOP-PUMP-07.

## 2 Isolation Points

The cooling water supply to P-12 is isolated at valve CW-V-112 on the pipe rack above the pump. The Y-strainer CW-S-12 is downstream of CW-V-112 at floor level. The return line rejoins the header through check valve CW-V-113.

## 3 Strainer Cleaning

Close CW-V-112, open the strainer drain, remove the CW-S-12 basket and clean it. Strainer fouling at Plant A is mostly cooling tower scale; replace the basket gasket at every cleaning. Reopen CW-V-112 slowly and vent the strainer.

## 4 Jacket Flush

If flow stays below 1.2 m³/h after strainer cleaning, connect the flushing hose to the jacket drain point JD-12 and flush with demineralised water for 10 minutes.

## 5 Records

Record the cleaning in the Plant A maintenance system under functional location PA-CW-P12.
